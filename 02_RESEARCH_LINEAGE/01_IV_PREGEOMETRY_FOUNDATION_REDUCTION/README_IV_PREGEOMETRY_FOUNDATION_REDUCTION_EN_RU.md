# ENGLISH VERSION

## 1. Purpose

This directory preserves one continuous early research line in the development of ontology-first mathematics within SINTEZ.

The documents are retained not as the current canonical mathematical foundation, but as a provenance-preserving record of a real research development:

\[
\text{ontological foundation}
\rightarrow
\text{systematic reduction}
\rightarrow
\text{mathematical recognition}
\rightarrow
\text{Möbius audit of the obtained form}.
\]

The lineage is important because later canonical mathematics did not arise independently of this work. It emerged through correction, elimination, recentering and preservation of those results that survived subsequent Möbius audits.

## 2. Status Rule

The documents in this directory must be read under the following status:

> **HISTORICALLY VALID RESEARCH LINEAGE / NOT CURRENT CANON / PARTIALLY SUPERSEDED BY LATER RECENTERING**

This means:

1. the documents faithfully record the state of the research at the time they were written;
2. they contain genuine methodological and mathematical developments that remain important for provenance;
3. not every ontological identification or mathematical correspondence in them remains canonical today;
4. later ontology-first work refined or replaced several foundations, especially through factual-zero recentering, the clarification of Development, spatial unfolding, fractality, and the relation between new Whole and factual zero;
5. no statement in this directory overrides a later canonical document.

When a conflict exists, the later canonical ontology-first document has priority.

## 4. Lineage Node 0 — Ontological Foundation

### `FOUNDATION_OF_DIFFERENCE_ONTOLOGY_001.md`

This document establishes the admissible ontological starting boundary.

Its lasting methodological contributions include:

- the Primary Act is not an object or an event in presupposed time;
- Difference, Environment and Möbius are distinguishable aspects of one Primary Act, not a temporal sequence;
- time, space, object, set, sequence and causality may not be silently projected into the foundation before their own origin is derived;
- ontological foundation, ontological reduction, logical derivation, temporal sequence and physical theory must remain distinct;
- the order of description must not be confused with the order of ontological emergence.

This document is the ontological root of the present lineage.

Some formulations concerning Primordial Intention / Ethics were later refined and must not be treated as the final canonical typing of Ethics, Direction and Development.

## 5. Stage 001 — Methodological Recentered Reduction

### `IV_FOUNDATION_ONTOLOGICAL_REDUCTION_STAGE_001_v1.md`

Stage 001 performs the first systematic re-audit of earlier IV-pregeometry results under the Principle of Foundation.

Major results:

- strict separation of ontological result from external epistemic transcription;
- symbols such as equality, implication, intersection, vectors and arrows are not treated as internally derived mathematics unless their ontological necessity has been established;
- Origin is never left; what may be lost is recognizability of its invariant;
- Möbius is retyped from a literal return to origin into restoration/preservation of recognizability through difference;
- independent Ontological Closure is eliminated as redundant;
- the earlier theorem candidate
  \[
  \mathfrak M \land C_o \Longrightarrow \mathcal S_o^{\min}
  \]
  is withdrawn in its previous form;
- the claim that Möbius necessarily yields a group is explicitly not demonstrated;
- object, another object and object-time are investigated through ontological elimination.

Stage 001 marks the beginning of the strict research method later retained throughout ontology-first mathematics.

## 6. Stage 002 — Space, Description, Process and Relation

### `IV_FOUNDATION_ONTOLOGICAL_REDUCTION_STAGE_002_v1.md`

Stage 002 continues the same reduction without importing classical space or relation.

Major results:

- primary spatiality is investigated as preservation of mutual irreducibility of different object identities of one origin;
- time and space are assigned different ontological functions;
- Description is introduced minimally as **holding the distinguished as distinguished**;
- intrinsic description does not require an external observer;
- process recognizability requires preservation of its intrinsic trajectory/provenance, not only its final boundary;

The document also records an early convergence between ontology and an independently obtained trajectory-memory result, while explicitly avoiding the claim that this constitutes a completed derivation of quantum formalism.

### `IV_FOUNDATION_ONTOLOGICAL_REDUCTION_STAGE_003_v1.md`

- the minimal axioms of a category receive an ontology-first interpretation.

The strict status of the result is not “category exists inside ontology,” but:

\[
\text{ontology-first connectedness under directed change}
\leadsto
\text{minimal categorical form}.
\]

## 8. Stage 004 — Return, Irreversibility and Operational Witness

### `IV_FOUNDATION_ONTOLOGICAL_REDUCTION_STAGE_004_v1.md`

Stage 004 performs a Möbius audit of the categorical direction by testing whether invertibility follows automatically.

Major results:

- a morphism does not universally require an inverse;
- Möbius return is not categorical inversion;
- return to the same object identity can remain a nontrivial endomorphism with preserved process memory;
- strict invertibility depends on the criterion of distinguishability retained by the representation;
- group and groupoid appear only after controlled quotienting/loss of part of full provenance;
- the distinction between pure identity and nontrivial return is translated into an operational one-slot process witness;
- a minimal one-qubit protocol is given as an operational realization of this distinction.

This stage is important because it prevents the research line from silently importing group structure merely because return or reversibility is present.

- Circle/Möbius bifurcation as final ontology;
- Ontological Connectedness as the final canonical basis of all morphisms;
- the categorical construction as the unique or final mathematical form of SINTEZ ontology;

Those later results have priority where they retype earlier claims.

The governing rule of this directory is therefore:

> **Preserve the path, but do not confuse the path with the current canonical destination.**

## 5. Stage 001 — Методологическая рецентрированная редукция

### `IV_FOUNDATION_ONTOLOGICAL_REDUCTION_STAGE_001_v1.md`

Stage 001 выполняет первый последовательный повторный аудит ранних результатов IV-предгеометрии через Принцип основания.

Основные результаты:

- символы равенства, импликации, пересечения, векторы и стрелки не считаются внутренне выведенной математикой до установления их онтологической необходимости;

- ранняя теорема-кандидат
  \[
  \mathfrak M \land C_o \Longrightarrow \mathcal S_o^{\min}

  снимается в прежнем виде;
- утверждение о необходимом выводе группы из Möbius явно признаётся недоказанным;
- объект, другой объект и объектное время исследуются методом онтологического исключения.

Stage 001 отмечает начало строгого исследовательского метода, который позднее сохраняется в ontology-first математике.

### `IV_FOUNDATION_ONTOLOGICAL_REDUCTION_STAGE_004_v1.md`

Stage 004 выполняет Мёбиус-аудит категориального направления, проверяя, следует ли обратимость автоматически.

Основные результаты:

- приводится минимальный однокубитный протокол как операционная реализация этого различия.

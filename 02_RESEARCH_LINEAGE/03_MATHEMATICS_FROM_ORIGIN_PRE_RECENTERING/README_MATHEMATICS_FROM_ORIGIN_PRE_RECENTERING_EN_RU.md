## 1. Purpose

This directory preserves a coherent stage in the development of SINTEZ ontology-first mathematics immediately before the later recentering around factual zero.

The documents collected here show three successive functions:

1. construction of a broad ontology-first mathematical architecture;
2. compression of that architecture into a cleaner mathematical kernel;
3. an external structural audit against the classical zeta architecture.

The directory is therefore a **research-lineage record**, not a statement of the current canonical mathematics.

Its role is to preserve how the project reached the boundary from which the later zero-center recentering became necessary.

---

All three documents preserve the same basic firewall:

\[
\boxed{

\to

## 5. What this lineage established

Within its own historical horizon, this stage established a number of durable methodological and mathematical distinctions:

- classical mathematics must not become a hidden source of ontology-first derivation;
- abstraction is lawful only when suspended Differences remain non-operative for the retained invariant;
- mathematical recurrence does not imply ontological identity;
- Holding and provenance may survive loss of selected content;
- a structural midpoint can be derived before importing a classical coordinate interpretation;
- a global symmetry center does not by itself force each individual element onto that center;
- local and global zero mechanisms must not be conflated;
- structural correspondence does not license identity of objects or theories.

These results remain important for provenance even where their local formalization was later refined.
